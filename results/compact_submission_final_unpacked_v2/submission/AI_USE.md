# AI assistance in the technical work

OpenAI Codex assisted with interpreting the problem, discussing and deriving
models, drafting and debugging code, constructing synthetic tests, reviewing
calculations, and analysing experimental limitations. Several checks use
separately written implementations and independent AI-agent review.

These automated checks are not external peer review or an independent human
assessment. They do not establish that the participant has already verified
every formula, assumption, result, citation or source file. Chengxuan YUAN,
Team Lebmar (87), remains responsible for reviewing and approving the submitted
work and its AI disclosure.

This package preserves adverse results and numerical boundaries. AI assistance
does not turn empirical support checks into future risk guarantees, or repeated
use of development data into independent validation. The compact adapter only
reproduces a fixed model and does not fit or select a new one.

Data attribution and the supplied data licence are recorded in
`docs/DATA_NOTICE.md` and `LICENSE-DATA-CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt`. That data notice
does not apply a new open-source licence to the participant's original code.
